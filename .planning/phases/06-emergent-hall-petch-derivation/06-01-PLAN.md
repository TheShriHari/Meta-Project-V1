# Plan 06-01: Emergent Hall–Petch Derivation, $\tau^*_{\text{ref}}$ Calibration & Interactive Chart

## Objective
Implement and verify the Phase 6 Emergent Hall–Petch engine in Python (`src/hpsim/core/hallpetch.py`) and TypeScript (`web/src/core/hallpetch.ts`), calibrate $\tau^*_{\text{ref}}$ across all 4 materials against `data/literature/hall_petch_verified_fits.csv`, validate `V-09`, `V-10`, and `V-15`, verify cross-language golden parity, and integrate the interactive Hall–Petch visualization in `web/src/ui/App.tsx`.

## Tasks
1. **Task 1 (Red Tests):** Write failing Python unit/validation tests (`tests/test_hallpetch.py`) and TypeScript tests (`web/tests/test_hallpetch.test.mjs`) for source shut-off (`solve_pileup_with_source`), yield-onset bisection (`find_yield_onset`), grain-size sweep & OLS/bootstrap fitting (`run_hall_petch_sweep`), `V-09` log–log slope, `V-10` $k_y$ consistency, and `V-15` calibration/held-out evaluation.
2. **Task 2 (Core Implementation & Calibration):** Implement `src/hpsim/core/hallpetch.py` and `web/src/core/hallpetch.ts`, calibrate $\tau^*_{\text{ref}}$ for Aluminium, Copper, Pure Alpha-Iron, and Ferritic Steel, update `src/hpsim/materials/materials.yaml`, `web/public/data/materials.json`, `scripts/validate_data_acquisition.py`, and `tests/test_materials_loader.py`.
3. **Task 3 (Golden Fixtures & Parity):** Create `scripts/generate_phase6_fixtures.py` to generate `tests/golden/phase6_fixtures.json` and verify cross-language parity in `tests/test_hallpetch.py` and `web/tests/phase6_parity.test.mjs`.
4. **Task 4 (Web UI & Documentation):** Add the interactive Phase 6 Emergent Hall–Petch SVG chart & controls card to `web/src/ui/App.tsx` and `web/src/ui/styles.css`, update `docs/model.md`, `docs/phase6_report.md`, `openspec/changes/hpsim-static-hosting/tasks.md`, and `openspec/changes/hpsim-static-hosting/assumptions.md`.
